# Surge XT

Surge XT is a big, free synthesizer made by the Surge Synth Team (not by Spinus). Spinus downloads it
for you (see [Get free plugins](free-plugins.md)) or uses the copy from your system's packages. Add it
from the [Browser](browser.md) (**Instruments › Free open source** › **Surge XT**) or the Channel Rack's
**+ Add channel** menu.

Double-click the channel's name to open it. Spinus shows Surge XT's own window, exactly as the Surge
Synth Team designed it, like any other plugin. Everything in it works as described in
[Surge XT's own manual](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/manual-xt/).

## Presets

- The preset's name is at the top centre of Surge XT's window. The arrows next to it load the previous
  or next preset; click the name to choose from all of them, sorted into Surge XT's own categories:
  its factory presets, the third-party ones that come with it, and your own.
- **Save** next to the name saves your changes as a preset of your own (in Documents/Surge XT/Patches).
- Loading a preset replaces the sound on this channel. Ctrl+Z does not undo it; load the previous
  preset again instead.

If Surge XT has no presets, its sounds are missing: download Surge XT again from **Options › Get free
plugins**.

## Macros

Surge XT's 8 macros are the controls each preset's author chose as the most useful ones, such as
**Cutoff** or **Resonance**. They are in Surge XT's window, at the left of its modulation row near the
bottom. To automate one, right-click the channel's name in the Channel Rack and choose **Create
automation clip: instrument**; the macros are at the top of the list.

## MPE controllers

Surge XT follows an [MPE controller](piano-roll.md#mpe-controllers) without changing its settings: each
note bends on its own, and pressing and sliding arrive as Surge's **Poly AT** and **Timbre** modulation
sources. Many presets use them; in Surge XT's window you can route them to any setting.
